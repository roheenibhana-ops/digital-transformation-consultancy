Questions

Is it a bot? Will customers know?

It's an automated assistant, and it never pretends to be a person. It writes in your brand's voice, signs off as your business, and hands over to a real person when it should, or whenever a customer asks. Anything it can't answer gets flagged so someone on your team replies.

What if I don't use Jobber?

I work with the tools you already have. If it has an API, we can usually connect it, and I'll tell you straight in the audit if it can't. If you don't have a CRM, I'll set one up.

You do. It runs on your own accounts, and once the final invoice is paid, it's yours: the accounts, the prompts and the documentation. I keep admin access until then.
