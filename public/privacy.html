Privacy policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

Plan First Studio ("I", "me") helps owners of service businesses find where they lose time and money. This page explains what information the website collects and what I do with it. Questions or requests: [email].

What I collect

You give me this when you fill in the application form or the Leak Scorecard:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your business name and website
  • Your yearly revenue band and what is slowing your business down
  • Your scorecard answers and the score I show you

The form also sends the page you were on, the site you came from, the time you submitted, and any tracking tags in the link you clicked (for example utm_source or an ad click ID), so I know which channel a lead came from.

The leak calculator works in your browser. What you enter in it is not sent to me.

Cloudflare, which hosts this site, processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect against abuse. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google also receives your IP address when a page loads.

What I use it for

  • To reply to your application, review whether we are a fit, and arrange a call
  • To show you your scorecard result and send the report and emails you asked for
  • To see which channels bring in genuine enquiries
  • To run and secure the site

Email

If you complete the scorecard, I will email you your report and a short series of emails about fixing the leaks it found. If you apply for discovery, I will email you about your application. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also reply and ask me to stop. I will not buy or sell email lists.

Who else sees it

I do not sell your information. I use a small number of service providers to run the business: Cloudflare (hosting and the database that stores form submissions), an email service for sending messages, and a calendar tool if you book a call. They may only use your data to provide their service to me. I may also share information if the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

The site does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If I add an ad pixel or analytics tool later, I will update this page first.

How long I keep it

I keep your information while it is relevant to your enquiry or our work together, and delete it when you ask me to or once it is no longer useful.

Your choices and rights

You can ask me to show you the information I hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email [email] and I will reply within 30 days.

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information I collect, to ask me to delete or correct it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. I do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

Children

This site is for business owners. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If I change this policy, I will update the date at the top.
